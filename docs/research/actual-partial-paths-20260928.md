Lohen also has a user-facing observed-resource ledger in `apps/web/src/calculator-character-resource-controls.ts`; it consumes recorded events, while leaving event observation and action scheduling explicit.

## Corrected formula and weapon status

The prior audit's claims that Alyosha and Lohen lacked formula handlers were based on looking only for named `sim-core` handlers. They are superseded: `apps/web/src/character-source-formulas.ts` defines `createAlyoshaA4BonusFormula` and `createLohenWillFormula`; `apps/web/src/character-calculations.ts` dispatches mapping formula IDs into those factories while constructing standard character Calculator documents. Lohen's resource panel is an additional observed-event route. The Alyosha observed-event ledger at `packages/sim-core/src/alyosha-observed-lifecycle.ts` now replays explicit C0 event histories and binds supported E/Q damage components to actual events; its UI is `apps/web/src/calculator-alyosha-observed-controls.ts`. Kachina's observed lifecycle editor is in `apps/web/src/calculator-kachina-controls.ts`. Both require user-entered observations and do not establish autonomous action timing or a complete rotation.

Eye of Perception has an explicit automatic fixed-gcsim implementation in `packages/sim-core/src/weapon-proc.ts`. It is separate from the 24 character partial IDs. The profile's pinned gcsim coefficient and callback behavior do not reconcile the conflicting catalogue/Optimizer multiplier and 50% trigger gate, so preserve its source-provisional status and the conflict record in `docs/cross-validation/weapon-proc-remaining-runtime-audit.md`.
