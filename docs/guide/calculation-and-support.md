# Calculation and support

GIDPS exposes distinct calculation paths:

- The independent calculator evaluates supported hits and skills from explicit input contexts.
- The TypeScript simulator executes configured action timelines and records damage, application, energy, and state changes.
- Pinned Classic and Stellar source hosts execute their respective source-engine rules and expose captured results through the application.

A result should be interpreted within its chosen path, data snapshot, reaction assumptions, and required conditions. A single-hit model, a complete skill cast, and a team rotation are different scopes.

## Support evidence

Catalog identity, a classified action, executable source code, a passing regression, and game-verified behavior are separate evidence levels. Read the action's support metadata and missing-condition controls. Do not infer complete support from a character count or a registered source package.

Tests preserve explicit contact, hit-count, duration, snapshot, constellation, and energy conditions. Unknown conditions should not silently become true. A valid short example should not be multiplied into an assumed long rotation.

## Inspecting results

Use formulas and intermediate values for individual calculations. For timelines, inspect ordered events, damage and energy records, and the associated action identities. Check cooldowns, resource availability, target assumptions, and actual simulation duration when comparing builds.

Historical result formats are frozen contracts. If an older format cannot represent a new semantic feature, compatibility must reject that conversion rather than remove the distinguishing field. Historical regression fixtures should come from their pinned historical implementation.

## Current boundaries

Mechanism and equipment coverage remains conditional. Full-account import, arbitrary teams, and all character/weapon combinations have not received universal acceptance. See the repository's [known limitations](../known-limitations.md), source-specific evidence, and current PR checks for narrower supported claims.
